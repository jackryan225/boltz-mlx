Boltz-2 on Apple MLX

Predictions from the MLX port of Boltz-2 on thirty PDB chains released after the training cutoff (single-sequence mode, no MSA), overlaid on the experimental structures. For the first ten, the MLX run used the same featurized inputs and diffusion noise as the PyTorch CPU reference, so the two predictions are the same structure to within a few thousandths of an ångström.

MLX prediction, coloured by pLDDT (low → high) experimental (superposed on Cα)
CA-lDDT and TM-score against the deposited model; parity is the all-atom RMSD between MLX and PyTorch predictions without superposition. Structures rendered with Mol*.
