# PREREG_MACBENCH: like-for-like Mac benchmark of the MLX port against upstream Boltz on PyTorch MPS

_Public copy. Edited only to remove local paths, internal job-queue tooling and internal role names (now "we" /
"the maintainer"); no rule, threshold, arm, case or number was changed. References to `RESULTS.md`,
`PREREG.md` and `docs/BOLTZ_MAC_LANDSCAPE.md` are to development documents that are not part of this repository; the
results they supported are summarised in [../report.md](../report.md)._

Written 2026-10-31 on branch `boltz-macbench` (base = `m5max` 35c397a = tag `r2-m5max-4`, `boltz_mlx` tree `b3a0ef2`), **before any
timing or accuracy data of this benchmark exists**. Nothing in this file may be loosened after data; a failed rule is reported as
failed. Deviations go at the end under "Deviations" with date and reason, and are repeated in the report. Machine: Apple M5 Max,
128 GB (hw.memsize 137438953472), the same machine for every arm. Every number a later document quotes is MEASURED (a log exists) or
ESTIMATE (rule given); published numbers are quoted with their URL and are never mixed into a measured column.

## 1. What is being claimed, and what is not

1. **Speed.** On this machine, with identical inputs, how long does the MLX build take compared with what a Mac user gets from upstream
   Boltz on PyTorch MPS (the configuration ChimeraX installs)? The published ChimeraX numbers are on other chips (M1 / M1 Max / M2 Ultra),
   with steps and the MSA accounting unstated, so they are shown beside our rows, not divided into them.
2. **Same predictions at our reduced step counts.** Do the Standard-preset settings (20 structure steps, 25 affinity steps) and the
   50-step setting give predictions that agree with upstream's own 200-step predictions as well as upstream's seeds agree with each other,
   and are they as close to the experimental structure?
3. Not claimed: any ranking against CUDA, any claim about inputs other than the cases below, anything about the ligand screen beyond the
   two T400 jobs of section 8.

"Standard preset": `boltz_mlx/screen/presets.py` has no preset named Standard. The shipped default on the NAX path (M5-class GPUs) is
`screen` (`DEFAULT_PRESET = "screen"`): `BOLTZ_MLX_PRECISION=f16`, 20 structure steps, 3 recycles, 25 affinity steps, 5 affinity recycles,
5 affinity samples, `validated=True`. This document takes "Standard" to mean `screen` (confirmed in the file on 2026-10-31).

## 4. Timing definitions

For every run (one process, fresh, started by `tools/macbench/driver.py`):

* **W (whole-process wall)**: monotonic wall seconds from just before the process is spawned to its exit, including interpreter start,
  imports, input processing and featurization, checkpoint loading, prediction, writing. Input files and MSAs are local (no network).
* **M (model-call time)**: MLX: the sum of `predict_<id>_s` and `affinity_<id>_s` from `boltz_mlx_timings.json` (the `model(...)` calls);
  upstream: the sum over `Boltz2.predict_step` calls with `torch.mps.synchronize()` before and after (`tools/macbench/up_predict.py`),
  split into structure and affinity. Checkpoint load times are reported separately where available.
* **Statistic**: median over the 3 seeds (n = 3, min and max shown); W is the headline. Seeds 0,1,2 for every arm.
* A run is "cold" in the sense that it is a new process; the file cache is not purged and runs within a (arm, input) group are
  consecutive, so the first run of a group may be slower; the median is the reported figure and all three are in the table.
* Every run records power source, battery %, system W, GPU utilization, thermal state, swap and memory level before and after
  (`tools/bench_r2/state.py`, read-only). A row not on AC power is labelled, not dropped and not repeated. A GPU idle of 120 s precedes each job.
* Speed ratio = median W (upstream `up200`) / median W (MLX arm) per input, labelled MEASURED; M ratios likewise. No threshold, no gate.
* Published numbers (ChimeraX help `runtimes.html`: Boltz 2.1.1 on M1 16 GB / M1 Max 32 GB / M2 Ultra 64 GB; PNCC workshop; MoleculeDesk
  M3) are listed beside as PUBLISHED with URL and never enter a ratio. Their stated and unstated settings are in
  `docs/BOLTZ_MAC_LANDSCAPE.md`.

## 5. Accuracy rules ("same predictions at our reduced step counts")

Data per (input, arm): the 3 seeds' structure sample 0 (CA of protein chains), confidence JSON, affinity JSON. Metrics are
`tools/validation/score.py`'s (the project's existing definitions: CA-lDDT global, radius 15 A, thresholds 0.5/1/2/4 A; TM-score with
Zhang-Skolnick superposition search, d0 from the reference length), computed by `tools/macbench/accuracy.py`:

* **Complex structure similarity** of two predictions X, Y: protein CA of all chains concatenated in YAML chain order, `score_arrays(X, Y)`;
  for identical-sequence chains (9moj A/B and C/D, ntca A/B) the within-entity chain order of X that maximises TM is used (all within-entity
  permutations, at most 4). Reported: TM and CA-lDDT. (DNA and ligands are not scored structurally; affinity and ligand confidence below.)
* **Resampling baseline** R_c: mean over the 3 unordered pairs of upstream seeds of the similarity (separately TM and lDDT).
* **Cross similarity** C_c(A): mean over all 9 (candidate seed i, `up200` seed j) pairs.
* **Rule S (agreement with upstream-200), per input and per metric, on the dissimilarity 1 - sim**:
  `1 - C_c(A) <= max( 1.25 x (1 - R_c) , (1 - R_c) + 0.01 )`. The 1.25 ratio is the L3-L resampling margin of PREREG.md ("ratio of medians
  <= 1.25"); the 0.01 floor keeps the rule defined where upstream seeds are almost identical. Applied for A in {`screen`, `f16_50`} (the claim),
  and also for `strict200` and `f16_200` as controls (a failure of `strict200` against `up200` says the difference is the implementation,
  RNG or numerics, not the step count).
* **Rule E (as accurate as upstream against experiment)**, inputs with a reference only: E_c(A) = mean over seeds of TM and CA-lDDT of the
  prediction vs the experimental structure (chain-wise as above, normalised by the modelled experimental residues). Pass when
  `E_c(A) >= E_c(up200) - max(0.02, range of the 3 upstream seed values)`. Better is never a failure.
* **Rule C (confidence)**: for `complex_plddt`, `ptm`, `iptm` (inputs with more than one chain or a ligand), `confidence_score` and, for
  ligand inputs, `ligand_iptm`: the candidate's seed-mean lies in `[min, max]` of the 3 `up200` seeds widened by 0.02.
* **Rule A (affinity)**, inputs 8rf4aff, lig_aff, ntca_aff in both MSA arms (6 groups): for `affinity_pred_value` and
  `affinity_probability_binary`, with sigma_pool = sqrt(mean over the groups of the `up200` seed variance, n - 1) (floors 0.02 for the
  value, 0.01 for the probability) and D_g = seed-mean(A) - seed-mean(`up200`): `RMS_g(D_g) <= 1.25 x sigma_pool x sqrt(2/3)` (the A2'
  rule of PREREG.md: the sd of a difference of two 3-seed means is sigma sqrt(2/3)). Per-group values and signs are reported.
* **Integrity**: every run has its CIF, confidence JSON (and affinity JSON where asked), all values finite. A missing or non-finite output
  is a FAIL of that run and is reported with the arm's n.
* **Verdict**: for each candidate arm and each rule family (S, E, C, A) the table lists every input; the arm "matches upstream-200"
  only if every input passes every family. Failing inputs are named. n = 3 seeds per arm makes each rule coarse (the resampling baseline
  rests on 3 pairs); that is stated in the report, not compensated by changing a threshold.
* **Step-count effect isolated from the implementation**: the same rules S, C, A are also applied with `f16_200` as the reference arm
  (its own 3-seed resampling baseline) for `screen` and `f16_50`; reported next to the upstream version, not instead of it.

## 6. Jobs and pins

All timed jobs are class 1 (run alone). Each job: pinned to branch `boltz-macbench`, `HEAD:boltz_mlx` = `b3a0ef2` (the `r2-m5max-4` tree),
clean code tree, blobs of the driver and helper scripts, the MSA manifest, upstream source HEAD `2be3519`, venv package versions;
exit 3 on mismatch (the job wrapper; not included in this repository). Jobs ran one at a time, started by the maintainer. Declared `expected_peak_gb` are
ESTIMATES with margin (MLX: MEASURED 8.8 GB peak footprint on the T400 screen, strict prot800 / prot1000 about 11-12 GB of activations in
RESULTS.md; upstream MPS fp32: ESTIMATE from the ChimeraX help page, which predicts 911 tokens on a 32 GB M1 Max). No case is expected above
80 GB. The driver stops the job after the current run when a run's peak footprint exceeds 80 GB or swap has grown by more than 2 GB since
the job started (cases are ordered small to large; the stop is logged and the exit code is 0 with `STOPPED_BY_GUARD` in the log).

## 7. Output layout and summary

`logs/macbench/<tag>/<arm>/<input>/seed<k>/` holds `run.json` (W, M, state, peak footprint from `/usr/bin/time -l`, command, versions),
`out/` (CIF, confidence and affinity JSON; PAE / PDE / pLDDT npz and processed features are deleted after the run). `tools/macbench/summary.py`
turns all runs into one table (MEASURED / ESTIMATE / PUBLISHED labels) and `tools/macbench/accuracy.py` evaluates section 5.

## 8. The two T400 jobs

* **`mb-t400-mlx`**: one full cold screen of the 199 T400 ligands (`data/external/fep/t400_ligands.csv`, lig_aff target + `seq1.a3m`) at
  `--preset screen --seed 0`, on tree `b3a0ef2`, reusing `tools/bench_r2` (`state.py`, `summary.py t400cold`, `expectations.json`): wall =
  process start to `results.csv`, power source per ligand row. Information only. ESTIMATE before data: 6.8 s/ligand (G1 pooled 6.835,
  4 cold passes 6.32-7.21 on an earlier tree), about 23 min, peak footprint about 10 GB.
* **`mb-t400-up-5` (+ conditional chunks)**: upstream `up200` settings on the same ligands, seed 0, YAML per ligand identical to what the screen
  writes (`target.ligand_yaml(smiles_used)`). First the first 5 ligands in one run; from its stderr timestamps and per-call spans
  the job computes `est_wall_199 = (W5 - span5) + 199 x span5 / 5` (span5 = first to last model-related activity of the 5-ligand run).
  **Decision, fixed now**: if `est_wall_199 < 21600 s` (6 h) the conditional chunk jobs (49-50 ligands each, `depends_on` the 5-ligand job) run the
  remaining 194 ligands; otherwise they exit 0 at once with a `SKIPPED (estimate X s >= 21600 s)` line and the measurement stays the 5-ligand set,
  labelled as such. The estimate is an ESTIMATE; the 5-ligand wall is MEASURED. Expectation before data (PREREG.md A5 budgeted MPS at about
  145 s per ligand for structure + affinity, an ESTIMATE, not a measurement): est about 8 h, so the 5-ligand set is the expected outcome.

*BCT diagnostic (added 2026-11-03, before any data of it)*: job `bct-diag` (8gpp only; arms f16_50, f16_200, screen seeds 1 and 2, strict20 = fp32 at 20 / 25 steps; one run each after an 8eil warm-up) is a diagnostic of the collapsed BCT in the Fast prediction, n = 1 per arm, no gate. Definitions fixed here: a **collapse** is any pair of ligand heavy atoms closer than 1.0 A in the predicted ligand (`nim_accuracy.py` section 4, `n_pairs_below_1A` > 0); **pose error** is the ligand heavy-atom RMSD against the experimental ligand after pocket-CA superposition (`nim_accuracy.py` section 3). Reference: `screen` seed 0 collapsed (6 of 6 pairs, largest distance 0.69 A, ligand RMSD 14.46 A), `strict200` and boltz-community did not (smallest pair 1.26 and 1.25 A; RMSD 1.84 and 1.65 A).
*Re-timing at the new Fast (added 2026-11-03, before any data of it)*: the Fast preset was changed to f16 + 50 structure + 25 affinity steps (the 20-step setting collapsed the 8gpp BCT in every run, see the BCT diagnostic). Job `nim-ours-rep50` (arm `f16_50_aff25`: f16, 50 structure steps, 25 affinity steps; not `f16_50`, whose affinity pass is 50 steps) re-times the same 4 NVIDIA-size cases with the inputs, MSAs, ligands, seed, warm-up, 3 interleaved rounds, limits and reporting (median and min-max per case for structure s and affinity s) of `nim-ours-rep`; timing only, no accuracy rule is added. The step counts are passed explicitly, as for every arm of this benchmark, so the benchmark tree `b3a0ef2` (whose `screen` preset still has 20 structure steps) runs it unchanged.
*Tiny-ligand re-timing (added 2026-11-03, before any data of it)*: Fast was restored to f16 + 20 structure + 25 affinity steps and added a rule (a ligand with < 10 heavy atoms: 200 structure steps, affinity 25); heavy atoms counted from the case YAMLs are 8eil SIN 8, 7qsj and 8cpk celecoxib 26, 8gpp BCT 4, so job `nim-ours-rep200` (arm `f16_200_aff25`: f16, 200 + 25 steps; not `f16_200`, whose affinity pass is 200 steps) times only 8eil and 8gpp with the inputs, MSAs, seed, warm-up (8eil), 3 interleaved rounds, limits and reporting of `nim-ours-rep`; timing only, no accuracy rule; 7qsj and 8cpk keep the 20-step numbers of `nim-ours-rep`.
